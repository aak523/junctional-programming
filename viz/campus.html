What each part is made of

PartGeometry EncodesScale & behaviour
PadCylinder r8 → r6.8, h2.6, 64 seg The site itselfReceives shadow; the only part that never moves. Carries the hit test on the globe.
RimTorus r8.2, tube 0.11 Site identityEmissive in the site's own colour — the one constant light when everything else is dark.
TowersBox grid, ⌈√slots⌉², step 11.36/cols Owned GPUs · lit = powered Height = kH × 3.0 world units, eased 16%/frame. Unlit towers sit in towerOff; lit ones take the site colour and pulse at 1.7 rad/s.
CrownsBox, 1.12 × 0.05 × 1.12 Gen NA cap on the tower. Gen N is twice the compute per thousand, so its tower is twice as tall for the same count.
SlabsBox 0.55 × 6, scaled in x Rented capacity Left of the pad: reservations, site colour at 34% opacity. Right: an exercised option, amber at 40%. Width = kH × 0.8; both bob on a 0.8 rad/s sine.
Pillars6 × open cylinder r0.16, h7 Spot fillsOne per 0.9 kH, on the pad's edge, flickering at 3 rad/s — there this month, gone the next.
SubstationBox 2.8², behind the pad Energised MWHeight = MW × 0.22. The wireframe ghost around it is power contracted but not yet energised — the gap you are waiting on.
Ground bar3 boxes, 0.4 × 3.0, scaled in x Served | training | unserved kH × 0.34 per segment, centred on the pad's front edge, with a tick at demand. The tick brightens when anything goes unserved.
Rings3 × torus r1, tube 0.22 Broker eventsA one-shot pulse: scales ×16 and fades over ~40 frames. Green delivered, red cancelled.

One source. The object is built in viz/campus3d.js and imported by both this bench and the globe, so a change here is a change there. Its contract is a single update(state, time) call per frame, taking owned and powered counts, rented kH, megawatts and the month's demand split — nothing about where it sits in the world.
